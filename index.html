The Issue

Even with the best of intentions, caregivers often struggle with:

Miscommunication

Keeping everyone on the same page with updates and changes

Complex Scheduling

Coordinating multiple caregivers, appointments, and daily tasks

Medication Management

Tracking medications, dosages, and administration schedules

Caregiving can be overwhelming for professionals, families and even the person being cared for.

  • Frequent care plan changes
  • Detailed conversations
  • Innumerable tasks
  • Orientation for caregivers
  • Difficult decisions
  • Medical appointments and more...
Our Solution

WholeCare provides an opportunity to simplify the overwhelming task of looking after someone.

Our platform brings together communication, scheduling, medication tracking, and care coordination into one intuitive system designed specifically for the unique challenges of caregiving.

Our Story

Necessity is the mother of invention

First-hand experience

WholeCare's founder has learned first-hand about the challenges and joys of caregiving.

A technically and emotionally complex job

Caregivers need to understand many practical details immediately, like scheduling and medications, without losing sight of the individual and their own particular needs.

The search for support

Having looked endlessly but unsuccessfully for a platform that would answer our needs, we decided to create our own.

Developing an intuitive system

We've distilled 20+ years of hands-on experience, down to the essentials - an intuitive system to manage communication and care for someone at home.

The WholeCare Mission

Our mission is to provide a communications hub and management platform, which offer seamless support for individuals and their families.

WholeCare makes it easy to sustain an individualized circle of care; allowing family, caregivers and healthcare providers to track their wellbeing and communicate effectively.
